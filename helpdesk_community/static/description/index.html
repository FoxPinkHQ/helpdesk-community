Helpdesk Community

Odoo 14 → 19 Community & Enterprise LGPL-3 · Free No external services

A complete, open-source support desk for Odoo — tickets, teams, pipeline stages, and a self-service customer portal. Production-ready and free, with zero artificial limits.

The problem

Support requests arrive by email, chat, and phone — and quietly get lost. Without a single place to track them, tickets slip through the cracks, customers repeat themselves, and your team has no idea what's actually pending. Most Odoo helpdesk options are either paid, locked to one Odoo version, or bloated with features you'll never use.

The solution

Helpdesk Community gives every request a home. Capture tickets, route them to the right team, move them through a clear pipeline, and let customers follow their own tickets through the portal — all inside Odoo, with no subscription and no third-party service. One clean module that runs on six Odoo versions (14 through 19).

Key features

Ticket management

Create, assign, prioritize, and resolve tickets with full chatter history and activity tracking.

Kanban pipeline

Drag-and-drop tickets across customizable stages: New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed.

Teams & agents

Group agents into teams and route tickets to the right people with per-team record rules.

Customer portal

Customers submit and track their own tickets — no agent login, no back-and-forth email.

Categories

Classify tickets (Bug, Feature, Billing, General) for faster triage and cleaner reporting.

Email notifications

Automatic messages on assignment and stage changes via native Odoo mail templates.

Access control

Three roles — User, Team Leader, Manager — with sensible defaults out of the box.

Search & filters

Group and filter by stage, team, priority, or category to find anything in seconds.

Screenshots

Kanban pipeline — drag tickets across stages, color-coded by priority.

Ticket form — everything about a request in one place, with chatter and activities.

List view — scan and bulk-manage tickets with sortable columns.

Powerful search — filter and group by stage, team, priority, or category.

Teams — organize agents and assign ticket ownership.

Stages — tailor your support pipeline to how your team works.

Customer portal — customers see all their tickets and statuses at a glance.

Portal detail — customers follow progress without contacting an agent.

Compatibility

One module. Six Odoo versions. Every release is built and tested on the full Odoo range — a level of coverage most helpdesk modules simply don't offer.
